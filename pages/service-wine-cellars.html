Atelier Engineering Standards

Uncompromising Joinery Built For Decades

Fine vintage wine requires strict environmental discipline: constant 55°F temperature, 65% relative humidity, zero vibration, and protection from ultraviolet light. TimberCraft builds architectural sommelier cellars from the vapor envelope to the hand-carved bottle racks. We utilize All-Heart California Redwood and Spanish Cedar—woods that naturally withstand high relative humidity without warping, harboring mold, or releasing aromatic terpenes that could taint vintage wine corks.

The TimberCraft Material Guarantee

Every timber slab is dried to 7-9% equilibrium moisture content in our computer-monitored kiln chambers before machining. We utilize non-toxic low-VOC adhesives and architectural-grade catalyzed finishes that withstand UV exposure, moisture, and daily household wear without yellowing or flaking.
