Staircase Architecture

Cantilever Staircases: Concealed Steel Anchors & Deflection Limits

By Marcus Vance (Master Joiner) • Published Oct 14, 2026 • 8 min read

Cantilevered floating staircases appear to defy gravity, projecting out from sheer plaster walls as individual floating timber slabs. However, what homeowners see as a delicate 60mm solid oak tread is actually the outer skin of an intricate structural steel cantilever system designed to absorb massive point-loads and dynamic foot-traffic vibration without detectable bounce.

1. The Hidden Structural Spine

Before drywall or timber treads are installed, our engineers fabricate a heavy 150mm x 100mm rectangular hollow section (RHS) steel stringer. This spine is either chemically anchored into a reinforced concrete shear wall or welded between structural steel floor columns. Projecting out from the spine are machined steel box spigots that sleeve deep inside each wooden tread.

Tread Projection Internal Steel Spigot Dynamic Point Load Deflection at Tip
900mm (Standard Villa) 70x50x6mm Heavy RHS Steel 300 kg (660 lbs) < 0.8mm (Virtually imperceptible)
1,100mm (Grand Residence) 90x60x8mm High-Tensile Steel 400 kg (880 lbs) < 1.2mm
1,300mm+ (Boutique Hotel) Tapered Gusseted Web Assembly 550 kg (1,210 lbs) < 1.5mm (L/500 code compliant)

2. 60mm Solid Quarter-Sawn Oak Tread Sleeves

Each wooden tread is hollowed on a 5-axis CNC router with laser-calibrated tolerances of ±0.2mm to slide over the steel spigot like a glove. We exclusively utilize Quarter-Sawn European White Oak or American Walnut. Quarter-sawing aligns vertical growth rings, reducing tangential expansion by over 50% compared to flat-sawn lumber and eliminating squeaks and binding against the steel core.

3. Recessed 2700K LED Channels & Structural Glass

Every tread features a recessed aluminum extrusion routed flush into the bottom leading edge, housing high-CRI (95+) 2700K warm LED ribbons behind opal polycarbonate diffusers. The balustrade is fabricated from 17.52mm low-iron toughened laminated architectural glass, clamp-fixed with concealed base shoe channels that provide code-compliant lateral guarding without visible stanchions.

Acoustic Decoupling Secret

Direct metal-to-timber contact produces transmission creaks over time. At TimberCraft, each internal steel spigot is fitted with a 2mm high-density chloroprene acoustic dampening boot before the timber sleeve is vacuum-pressed into place. This decouples footsteps completely, guaranteeing whisper-quiet ascents.
