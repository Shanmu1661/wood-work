Architectural Staircases Case Study

Aethelgard Floating Cantilever Staircase

Bel-Air, California • Timeline: 8 Weeks • 60mm Solid Quarter-Sawn White Oak
Location Bel-Air, California
Architect / Partner Elysium Architectural Design
Primary Timber 60mm Solid Quarter-Sawn White Oak
Calibration Standard AWI Premium ±0.5mm

The Architectural Challenge

A contemporary luxury villa required a floating staircase with zero visible wall stringers, no floor columns, and absolutely imperceptible deflection or bounce when descending, paired with a frameless structural glass balustrade.

TimberCraft Atelier Solution

We embedded a 150mm x 100mm structural steel RHS spine directly into the reinforced concrete shear wall during framing. 20 machined cantilever steel spigots were welded to the spine. We routed solid 60mm Quarter-Sawn White Oak tread box sleeves on a 5-axis CNC with internal 2mm acoustic decoupling boots to slide over each steel spigot. Recessed 2700K warm LED channels illuminate every step.

Joinery Details & Craftsmanship Highlights

  • Sub-millimeter deflection tip engineering (L/500 structural code compliance)
  • Internal chloroprene acoustic decoupling boots eliminating metal creaks
  • Recessed under-tread 2700K warm LED strips with hidden conduit routing
  • Frameless 17.52mm low-iron glass balustrade anchored into base shoe channels
