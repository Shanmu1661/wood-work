Wardrobes & Dressing Suites Case Study

Minimalist Cedar-Lined Wardrobe Suite

Pacific Palisades, California • Timeline: 6 Weeks • Aromatic Red Cedar
Location Pacific Palisades, California
Architect / Partner Kaufmann & Partners
Primary Timber Aromatic Red Cedar
Calibration Standard AWI Premium ±0.5mm

The Architectural Challenge

The client desired floor-to-ceiling closets that appear as a smooth monolithic walnut wall when closed, while providing natural moth-repellent properties for fine designer clothing and shoes without synthetic chemicals.

TimberCraft Atelier Solution

We lined the entire internal carcass and drawers with hand-planed aromatic Red Cedar, leaving the cedar unfinished to maximize natural fragrance and moisture absorption. The 3.0m exterior walnut sliding doors were fitted with top-hung Hafele Slido tracking with concealed bottom guides and soft-closing dampers, eliminating floor tracks.

Joinery Details & Craftsmanship Highlights

  • Top-hung sliding hardware eliminating floor-level trip hazards and dust tracks
  • Natural aromatic cedar interior protecting silk, cashmere, and fine woolens
  • Concealed Hafele door straightener rods counteracting seasonal door warp
  • Continuous vertical walnut crown grain flow across 4 expansive sliding panels
