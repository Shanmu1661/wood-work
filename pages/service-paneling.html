Uncompromising Joinery Built For Decades

Contemporary spaces with extensive glass and polished concrete often suffer from excessive flutter echoes and hollow acoustics. TimberCraft transforms surfaces with precision-fluted acoustic timber battens mounted over 9mm high-density acoustic PET felt. Each batten is machined from kiln-seasoned European white oak or American black walnut, with micro-chamfered edges that diffuse high-frequency reflections while the felt absorbs ambient bass frequencies. We also build secret flush jib doors that vanish seamlessly into the slat pattern.

The TimberCraft Material Guarantee

Every timber slab is dried to 7-9% equilibrium moisture content in our computer-monitored kiln chambers before machining. We utilize non-toxic low-VOC adhesives and architectural-grade catalyzed finishes that withstand UV exposure, moisture, and daily household wear without yellowing or flaking.
