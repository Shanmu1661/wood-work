Wardrobes & Dressing Suites Case Study

Bel-Air Villa Walk-in Wardrobe Suite

Bel-Air, California • Timeline: 8 Weeks • Quarter-Sawn European White Oak
Location Bel-Air, California
Architect / Partner Studio Veloce Architecture
Primary Timber Quarter-Sawn European White Oak
Calibration Standard AWI Premium ±0.5mm

The Architectural Challenge

The client required a 750 sq.ft primary dressing room with ceiling heights varying from 3.1m to 3.4m across sloping structural roof beams. Standard modular wardrobes left awkward dead spaces and would not support full-length mirror sliding leaves without binding.

TimberCraft Atelier Solution

TimberCraft conducted a sub-millimeter 3D Faro laser survey to map every beam deflection. We fabricated floor-to-ceiling carcasses using IS 710 BWP plywood cores with continuous sequence-matched European White Oak exterior veneers. Behind the doors, internal shelving features concealed 2700K LED routing with capacitive touch dimmer switches and aromatic cedar drawer linings to naturally protect cashmere garments.

Joinery Details & Craftsmanship Highlights

  • Sequence-matched vertical oak grain extending across 6 adjacent doors
  • Integrated shadow gap ventilation ensuring zero condensation against exterior villa walls
  • Hand-assembled aromatic cedar dovetailed jewelry trays with velvet inserts
  • Dual Hafele concealed tensioner rods embedded into each 3.2m door leaf
