Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our 3D laser site surveys, timber kiln seasoning, turnaround schedules, and 10-year master joinery warranty.

Our Senior Joinery Consultant visits your home with Leica 3D laser scanners to measure exact wall plumb, floor pitch, and electrical socket locations. We also bring physical hardwood swatches (Burma Teak, Walnut, White Oak, Rosewood) and physical hardware samples so you can see finishes under your actual ambient lighting. Within 48 hours, you receive a full 3D CAD schematic and an itemized fixed quote.

Wood warps when raw timber is not properly seasoned or when interior moisture fluctuates. TimberCraft strictly uses kiln-dried lumber moisture-tested to 8-10% prior to milling. For large door leaves and paneling, we insert internal steel tension stabilizer bars (straightening rods) and employ quarter-sawn cut veneers that expand and contract predictably without twisting.

Single installations (like a floating TV console or pivot door) typically require 2 to 3 weeks in our workshop. Multi-room fitouts (master wardrobe suite + acoustic paneling + modular kitchen) take 4 to 6 weeks. Because we dry-assemble and pre-fit all joinery in our facility, on-site installation at your property usually takes just 1 to 3 days with minimal disruption.

We offer a comprehensive 10-Year Structural Joinery Warranty against carcass delamination, joint looseness, or timber defects. Furthermore, all Austrian Blum hinges, drawer runners, and motorized lift mechanisms carry a lifetime manufacturer warranty.
