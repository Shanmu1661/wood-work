Kitchen Joinery & Islands Case Study

Heritage Teak Island with Dovetail Drawers

Santa Barbara, California • Timeline: 5 Weeks • Burma Teak
Location Santa Barbara, California
Architect / Partner Coastline Architectural Studio
Primary Timber Burma Teak
Calibration Standard AWI Premium ±0.5mm

The Architectural Challenge

Crafting a sculptural culinary island that combines a professional chef’s heavy end-grain butcher block workstation with furniture-grade teak cabinetry and brass footings.

TimberCraft Atelier Solution

Our master woodworkers selected sustainably harvested old-growth Burma Teak for its natural resistance to water, acids, and cooking oils. All 12 drawer boxes were constructed with hand-cut through dovetail joinery in solid Hardrock Maple. The prep workstation features a 100mm thick end-grain maple butcher block finished with natural food-safe organic beeswax.

Joinery Details & Craftsmanship Highlights

  • Hand-cut through dovetail joints exposed on all 12 drawer fronts
  • 100mm thick end-grain maple prep butcher block integrated seamlessly
  • Solid hand-machined brass leg sleeves and levelling feet
  • Concealed Blum undermount soft-close runners rated for 50kg per drawer
