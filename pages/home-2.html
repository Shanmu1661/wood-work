Execution Protocol

From Blueprint Submittal to Turnkey Site Handover

Our disciplined 5-stage protocol ensures zero on-site delays, eliminated RFIs, and flawless coordination with MEP and GC trades.

01

3D Laser Site Survey

We deploy Faro 3D laser scanners to create million-point clouds of the existing slab, plumb lines, and ceiling elevations.

02

BIM LOD 350+ Modeling

Comprehensive Revit submittals, full section details, hardware schedules, and PE-stamped structural anchorage calculations.

03

5-Axis CNC Milling

State-of-the-art Italian CNC milling machines cut flutes, mortises, and curved radius forms to ±0.2mm tolerance.

04

Mockup Bench Review

Full-scale joinery mockups and finished timber flitches delivered to the architect's office or reviewed at our Portland atelier.

05

Certified Installation

OSHA-30 union & non-union installation crews handle white-glove site erection, scribing, and final punch-list commissioning.
