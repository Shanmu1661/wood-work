Uncompromising Joinery Built For Decades

A staircase is not merely a passage between floors; it is a room's primary architectural sculpture. TimberCraft engineers cantilever floating stairs where each 60mm thick solid hardwood tread appears to hover weightlessly out of the drywall. The secret lies behind the wall: a heavy-gauge structural steel carriage mechanically anchored into the building's structural framework, engineered to eliminate all bounce, creaks, and deflection under high dynamic loads.

The TimberCraft Material Guarantee

Every timber slab is dried to 7-9% equilibrium moisture content in our computer-monitored kiln chambers before machining. We utilize non-toxic low-VOC adhesives and architectural-grade catalyzed finishes that withstand UV exposure, moisture, and daily household wear without yellowing or flaking.
