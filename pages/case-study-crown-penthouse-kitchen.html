Kitchen Joinery & Cabinetry Case Study

Crown Penthouse Solid Oak Kitchen

Tribeca, New York • Timeline: 9 Weeks • European White Oak
Location Tribeca, New York
Architect / Partner Tribeca Design Studio
Primary Timber European White Oak
Calibration Standard AWI Premium ±0.5mm

The Architectural Challenge

A high-rise penthouse kitchen required seamless handleless cabinetry capable of resisting high culinary steam and moisture while maintaining crisp shadow line gaps of exactly 3mm across expansive island frontages.

TimberCraft Atelier Solution

We engineered carcasses using 18mm Russian Birch BWP plywood with waterproof PUR edge-banding. Doors were crafted from rift-sawn European White Oak veneers bonded under vacuum press to moisture-resistant cores with rear balancing backers. Austrian Blum Legrabox drawers with motorized Servo-Drive open at the touch of a knee, even with full loads of cast iron cookware.

Joinery Details & Craftsmanship Highlights

  • 100% boiling-waterproof marine birch plywood carcass construction
  • Motorized Servo-Drive touch-to-open drawer mechanisms throughout
  • Concealed integrated appliance frontages for Miele & Sub-Zero units
  • Integrated quartz waterfall island apron joinery with brass shadowline
