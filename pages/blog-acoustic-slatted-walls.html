Acoustic Engineering

Acoustic Slatted Walls: PET Felt Backing & NRC Absorption

By Marcus Vance (Master Joiner) • Published Sep 29, 2026 • 7 min read

Contemporary architectural villas with double-height ceilings, polished concrete floors, and expansive glass curtain walls suffer from severe acoustic reverberation. Conversations echo uncomfortably, and television audio turns muddy. While commercial acoustic foam baffles look like industrial recording studios, acoustic timber slatted paneling introduces organic warmth while functioning as high-performance sound absorption arrays.

1. The Physics of Timber Slat Sound Absorption

Acoustic slatted panels work as a hybrid absorber-diffuser system. When sound waves hit the panel, the sequence-matched hardwood slats break apart specular sound reflections and diffuse mid-to-high frequencies. Concurrently, sound energy penetrates through the 13mm air gaps between the slats and is absorbed into the porous fibrous backing through viscous boundary-layer friction.

2. The 9mm High-Density Recycled PET Felt Substrate

Rather than gluing slats onto acoustic fiberglass or mineral wool which can release irritating airborne fibers, we mount our CNC-profiled slats onto 9mm thermo-bonded, recycled polyethylene terephthalate (PET) felt. This substrate is completely non-toxic, odorless, mold-resistant, and 100% recyclable. Its micro-cellular structure delivers an overall Noise Reduction Coefficient (NRC) rating of 0.85 when installed flush, or 0.95 when mounted over a 45mm timber batten cavity.

3. Sequence-Matched Flitches & ASTM E84 Fire Testing

Each slat measures 27mm wide by 11mm deep, sliced from consecutive flitches of American Black Walnut or Quarter-Sawn White Oak so cathedral grain flows uninterrupted across the entire width of your feature wall. All panels destined for commercial boardrooms or luxury residences are finished with Class-A intumescent clear sealers compliant with ASTM E84 flame spread regulations without masking the warm tactile timber grain.

Invisible Fixing Tip

Never face-screw or nail through the decorative hardwood slats. Fixings should always be made using matte-black phosphate drywall screws driven directly through the black PET acoustic felt between the slats. The screw heads sink flush into the felt and disappear completely from view.
